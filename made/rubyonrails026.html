Why Ruby in 2026

In the AI era, Ruby on Rails continues to be a vibrant framework for developers building modern applications. Its elegant syntax, developer-friendly conventions, and growing AI ecosystem make it a compelling choice for startups, product builders, and AI-integrated projects.

Carmine Paolino — Creator of RubyLLM

Carmine is actively promoting AI integration in Rails through RubyLLM, providing developers a unified interface to large language models.

“RubyLLM gives you one beautiful API for all LLM providers… AI development has fundamentally shifted… and who better than the Ruby community knows how to build products people love?” Source
“Simple should be simple, complex should be possible… models and providers, they are commodities… we need to be able to change them really quickly.” Source

Paweł Strzałkowski — CTO, Visuality (Model Context Protocol)

Paweł is building tools that make Rails apps AI-native, allowing developers to scaffold AI interaction layers alongside traditional human-facing interfaces.

“Imagine Rails scaffolding not just views for humans, but a parallel, AI interaction layer… make your app speak AI natively.” Source

Joe Leo — Ruby Community Commentator

Joe emphasizes productivity and low cognitive load when integrating AI into Ruby projects, highlighting how RubyLLM simplifies workflows for developers.

“RubyLLM lets me think less about the tool and more about the work… it keeps the mental load low so developers can stay present with the work.” Source

Garry Tan — President, Y Combinator

Garry highlights the synergy between Rails’ developer-friendly conventions and AI, suggesting Rails is well-positioned to take advantage of LLMs and AI-enhanced productivity tools.

“I think people are sleeping a bit on how much Ruby on Rails + Claude Code is a crazy unlock — I mean Rails was designed for people who love syntactic sugar, and LLMs are sugar fiends.” Source

Ruby in 2026 is not just about building apps quickly — it’s about integrating AI naturally, maintaining developer happiness, and leveraging community-driven tools to create scalable, future-ready applications.
