Founder's Story — ToTravelTo

A short, practical account of building a rail-focused travel startup in Europe.

Lessons, tradeoffs and the product choices we made while exploring low-carbon travel and founder opportunities.

Read the story Visit site ↗

Why ToTravelTo

ToTravelTo started as a simple question: can you make intercity rail travel easier and more appealing in Europe? We focused on practical product experiments, partnerships and rapid prototypes rather than big-bang engineering.

Early experiments

We validated demand with targeted landing pages and low-cost ticket-finding prototypes. The goal was always to learn quickly: what booking friction mattered, where partnerships unlocked inventory, and which user stories could be solved without heavy integrations.

Product choices

  • Keep flows minimal — reduce decision points for users.
  • Favor integrations that are stable and low-maintenance.
  • Design for accessibility and predictability across countries.

Business & distribution

Rail has complex distribution — we learned early that strong operator relationships and clear UX patterns matter more than marginal feature improvements.

Where we are now

The project is currently paused; parts of the tech and research remain useful for future initiatives.
